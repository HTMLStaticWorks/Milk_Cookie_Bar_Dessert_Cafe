Our Sweet Story

Discover the passion behind every bite and the journey

that brought us to your neighborhood.

Est. 2018

Born From a Love of Baking

It all started in a tiny home kitchen with a simple mission: to create the perfect chocolate chip cookie that would remind everyone of home.

What began as a weekend pop-up experiment quickly turned into a neighborhood obsession. We spent months perfecting our dough, sourcing the richest European butter, and testing dozens of chocolate origins to find the ideal melt-point.

"Every cookie is a little piece of home."

Today, Milk & Cookie Bar is more than just a bakery. it's a sanctuary for dessert lovers, a place where warm bakes and cold milk flights create moments of pure, unadulterated joy.

Quality Ingredients

We never compromise on our core components. From organic farm milk to Belgian chocolate, only the best makes the cut.

Made With Love

Baking is personal for us. Each batch is mixed, scooped, and baked by hand with the same care we'd give our own families.

Community First

Our bakery is a hub for connection. We support local farmers and strive to be a positive force in our neighborhood.
